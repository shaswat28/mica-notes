The basics

What is a thread?

A thread holds one idea and every note you add to it over time. Start it with one note, then add more to the same thread whenever something new comes to mind. Notes line up on a timeline, each with its own time.

Which devices does Mica Notes run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later, and Mac with macOS 26 or later. On Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later, there’s a voice recorder that sends recordings to your iPhone. AI features and transcription also depend on your device and language.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can write, read, record and search without a connection. Changes sync the next time you’re online. Link previews need a connection, and transcription may need a one-time language download from Apple.

How do I search for something specific?

Type in the search field. You can add filters to narrow results:

  • tag:ideas for threads with a tag
  • has:link, has:photo or has:voice
  • is:pinned
  • after:2026-01-31 or before:2026-03-01

Search also matches by meaning, so a note can turn up even when you don’t remember the exact words.

How do reminders work?

Tap the bell in a thread’s toolbar and type a time in plain words, like “tomorrow 9am”, or a repeating one, like “every friday 9am”. Turn on Time Sensitive for a reminder that should come through a Focus. Mica Notes asks for notification permission the first time you set one.

What is Review?

Review shows threads you haven’t opened in a while, one at a time, so you can keep, snooze or clear each one.

iCloud sync

Is sync free?

Yes. Sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac is part of the free app. Notes, tags, photos and voice recordings sync through your own private iCloud database, and their storage counts toward your iCloud storage.

How do I turn sync on or off?

Sync is on by default when you’re signed in to iCloud. Change it in Settings → iCloud Sync. Turning sync off keeps every note on that device, and notes already in iCloud stay there.

My notes aren’t showing up on another device.

Try these in order:

  1. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account in iCloud.
  2. Check that iCloud is allowed for Mica Notes in your device’s iCloud settings.
  3. Open Settings → iCloud Sync in Mica Notes on both devices. It shows the last sync time and a Retry button when something went wrong.
  4. Make sure there’s free iCloud storage. Large photos and recordings need room to upload.
  5. Give it a moment on a good connection. Photos and voice recordings can arrive a little after the note text.

If it still doesn’t catch up, email support with what the sync screen says on each device.

What happens if I sign in with a different iCloud account?

Mica Notes opens a separate library for that account, so two people’s notes never mix. You can keep your current library separate or copy its notes into the new one. Nothing is deleted when you switch.

Mica Plus

What does Mica Plus include?

Mica Plus is a one-time $4.99 purchase (or your local price). It unlocks:

  • the theme customizer and saved presets
  • five more themes: Oil slick, Ember, Lagoon, Dusk and Pearl
  • alternate app icons
  • AI tag suggestions, AI rules per tag and related threads, using Apple Intelligence on your device

Everything else is free, including sync and search by meaning.

I bought Plus. How do I get it on my other devices?

Open the Mica Plus screen in Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing is on, so people in your family group get Plus too.

Why don’t I see AI features?

They need Mica Plus, a device that supports Apple Intelligence, and Apple Intelligence turned on in your device settings. Check that the AI switch in Mica Notes Settings is on too. While Apple’s model is downloading or unavailable, the app keeps working without it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com. If Plus is refunded, your notes stay exactly as they are, and the app switches back to a free theme while keeping your custom theme settings.

Capture and voice

What are the quickest ways to add a note?
  • iPhone and iPad: Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center, the Action button, or Share → Mica Notes from another app.
  • Mac: press ⌃⌥N from any app for quick capture, or use the menu bar icon. You can change the shortcut in Settings.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: add a note without opening the app.
Why wasn’t a voice note transcribed?

Check that “Transcribe voice notes” is on in Settings. Transcription runs on your device, so it needs a supported device and language, and sometimes a language download from Apple. The recording is always saved and can still be played.

How does recording on Apple Watch work?

Record on the Watch and the recording waits there until it can reach your paired iPhone, even if the iPhone is out of range for a while. Once it’s in your library, it syncs to your other devices like any other note.

Your data

Can I get a deleted note back?

Look in Recently Deleted. Deleted notes stay there for 30 days unless you delete them for good sooner. Each note also keeps its edit history, so you can go back to an earlier version.

How do I keep a copy of my notes?

Export a thread or your whole library as Markdown, and turn on automatic backups in Settings. Backups and exports are saved where you choose. If that’s a cloud folder, the provider’s own terms apply to that copy.

Can I import notes from another app?

Yes, from Markdown files. On Mac choose File → Import Notes; on iPhone and iPad use Import in the library menu. Tags written as #tags come along too.

Can I lock the app?

Yes. Turn on App Lock in Settings to require Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode. While it’s locked, your notes are also kept out of Spotlight.

Does AI send my notes anywhere?

No. AI features use Apple Intelligence’s on-device model only, never a cloud or third-party AI service, and one switch in Settings turns them all off. The privacy policy covers everything else, including link previews.
