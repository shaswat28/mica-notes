How threads work

Ideas rarely show up all at once.

So you don’t have to finish a note in one go. A thread collects the pieces as they come, in order, each with the time you wrote it.

  1. Start a thread with a sentence, a link, a photo or a voice memo. On a Mac, ⌃⌥N opens a capture window from whatever app you’re in.

  2. The next time it comes up, add a note to the same thread. Everything about that idea stays in one place, with the time you wrote each part.

  3. Search looks through your notes, tags and the text in your photos. Review brings back threads you haven’t opened in a while.

Questions

Common questions

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can write, read, record and search without a connection, and changes sync when you’re back online.

What if I stop syncing?

Your notes stay on the device. Turning sync off, signing out of iCloud or getting a refund doesn’t delete anything.

Can I move my notes elsewhere?

Yes. Export a thread or everything as Markdown, and set up automatic backups in Settings.
