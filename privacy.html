Privacy

Privacy policy

Your notes stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. Mica Notes has no server of its own, no account system, no analytics, no ads and no third-party AI.

Effective November 22, 2026. Replaces the version of November 21, 2026.

The short version

  • Your notes are stored on your devices and, if sync is on, in your own private iCloud database. The developer can’t read them.
  • The app collects no analytics, shows no ads and contains no third-party tracking code.
  • AI features run on your device with Apple Intelligence. Note text never goes to a cloud or third-party AI service.
  • The only time the app contacts a website on its own is to fetch a link preview, which you can turn off.
  • The developer only receives what you choose to send, such as a support email.

Notes and iCloud sync

Your notes, tags, photos, voice recordings and related app data are stored on your device. When iCloud sync is on, they also sync through your own private iCloud database, which belongs to your Apple Account. Sync is on by default when you’re signed in to iCloud, and you can turn it off in Settings → iCloud Sync.

The developer runs no server for your notes and has no access to your private iCloud database. Apple stores and protects that data under its own privacy policy. Turning sync off keeps your notes on the device; notes already in iCloud stay there until you remove them.

Deleted notes stay in Recently Deleted for up to 30 days unless you delete them for good sooner. Each note keeps its edit history on your devices so you can restore an earlier version.

What happens on your device

AI features

Tag suggestions, AI rules per tag, related threads and search by meaning use Apple Intelligence’s on-device model or on-device processing. Your note text isn’t sent to any cloud or third-party AI service. One switch in Settings turns AI features off, and availability depends on your device and Apple Intelligence settings.

Voice and photos

Voice transcription and text recognition in photos run on your device. Transcription may need Apple to download language files the first time. Recording needs microphone permission, and you choose which photos and files to add.

Search, Spotlight and widgets

The search index is built and kept on your device. If “Show notes in Spotlight” is on, thread titles and text are added to your device’s Spotlight index; turning on App Lock removes them. Widgets, Control Center, Siri and Shortcuts read and write notes through storage shared only between Mica Notes and its own extensions on the same device.

Reminders and App Lock

Reminders are scheduled as local notifications on your device, and the app asks for notification permission first. App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode through Apple’s system authentication; the app never sees your biometric data or passcode.

Apple Watch

Recordings made on Apple Watch are kept on the Watch until they’re delivered to your paired iPhone through Apple’s Watch connectivity, then removed from the Watch.

Link previews

When a note contains a link, the app can fetch the page’s title and image to show a preview. That request goes straight from your device to the linked website, which receives the link address and your IP address and may log them under its own practices. The rest of your note is never sent. Previews are cached on your device and aren’t synced. You can turn off “Fetch link previews” in Settings.

Exports, backups and sharing

You decide whether and where to export, back up or mirror your notes. If you choose iCloud Drive or another cloud folder, that provider handles the copy under its own terms. Sharing a note to another app sends the content you selected to that app.

Purchases and Apple

Mica Plus is bought, restored and refunded through Apple. The developer never receives your payment details. Apple shares sales reports with developers that don’t identify you.

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device’s privacy settings, Apple may provide the developer with crash reports and usage statistics that Apple collects. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

This website

This site has no analytics, trackers, advertising, external fonts or cookies. Its only script runs the demo and animations in your browser and sends nothing anywhere. It’s hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log request data such as your IP address to serve and secure the site, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Your choices

  • See and export all your notes at any time with Export in the app.
  • Delete any note or thread in the app; it’s removed from your devices after Recently Deleted is emptied.
  • Remove iCloud data by deleting it in the app with sync on, or from your iCloud storage settings on your device.
  • Turn features off: iCloud sync, AI, link previews, Spotlight and transcription each have a switch in Settings.

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there’s nothing on our side to access, correct or delete, apart from any emails you’ve sent us.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. The only information the developer can receive is what’s described above: emails you choose to send, and Apple’s crash reports and usage statistics if you’ve opted in to sharing them.

Support email and contact

If you email micanotes.support@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and whatever you choose to include. Those messages are used only to answer you and are deleted on request. Please don’t send private note content unless it’s needed.

Questions about this policy go to the same address. If the way the app handles data changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
